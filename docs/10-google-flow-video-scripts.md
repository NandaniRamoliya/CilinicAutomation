# 10 · Google Flow video scripts (10-second promo reels)

Ready-to-paste prompts for **Google Flow** (Veo AI video), for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp
Status ads that sell the clinic automation to doctors.

## How to use these

- **One Veo clip is 8 seconds.** Each "10-second" reel below is **one 8-second clip + a 2-second end card**,
  which you add in CapCut, Canva or Flow's Scenebuilder. For longer ads, chain clips in Scenebuilder (see the 30-second ad at the end).
- **Aspect ratio:** pick **9:16 (portrait)** in Flow's settings if your model offers it; otherwise make 16:9 and crop to 9:16 in your editor.
- **Model:** use Veo 3 (it generates the speech and sound too). Use **Veo 3 Fast** for drafts, because it costs fewer credits.
- **Same faces in every clip:** create the three characters below once with *Ingredients to Video*
  (or generate one still image of each and reuse it). Paste the same character description into every prompt.
- **Dialogue:** keep it to one or two short lines, at most ~15 words in 8 seconds. Hinglish works best for Indian clinics.
- **On-screen text** (the `TEXT:` lines) is added in your editor. AI video often misspells written text, so don't ask Veo to draw words or app screens.
- **No brand logos:** the prompts describe "a green chat app notification", not WhatsApp's logo. Veo often refuses trademarks, and you should not show them in ads.

### Rules that keep your ads honest and allowed

- These are **dramatizations with AI actors**. Never present them as a real doctor's testimonial or a real clinic. Add a small "AI-generated / dramatization" label. Meta and YouTube require disclosure for realistic AI content.
- Don't promise numbers ("zero no-shows", "double your income"). Talk about what the system **does**.
- Never show diagnosis, medicines or reports on screen. That's the same privacy rule as the product itself.

## Reel 1 · "The empty chair" (problem hook)

**Goal:** stop the scroll with the pain of no-shows.

**Flow prompt:**
```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic shot. SETTING. The DOCTOR stands at the doorway of his consultation room holding a patient file,
looking at a row of empty steel waiting chairs. The wall clock shows 11 o'clock. Slow dolly-in on his disappointed face.
Soft natural light, shallow depth of field, realistic, 35mm film look.
He sighs and says in Hindi-English: "Teesra no-show... aaj phir slot khaali gaya."
Ambient sound: quiet clinic, ceiling fan humming, distant traffic.
```
**TEXT (0–3 s):** `Patient bhool gaya… slot khaali 😞`
**TEXT (5–8 s):** `Har no-show = time + paisa`

## Reel 3 · "Same slot, two patients" (double booking)

**Goal:** show the double-booking pain, then the calm alternative.

**Flow prompt:**
```
Vertical 9:16. SETTING. Two patients arrive at the reception desk at the same moment, both holding out their phones
and speaking at once. The RECEPTIONIST looks at a paper register in confusion, flipping pages, stressed.
Quick handheld camera, slightly chaotic energy, realistic.
Patient one says: "Mera appointment saade dus baje ka hai!" Patient two says: "Mera bhi!"
Sound: overlapping voices, phone ringing in the background.
```
**TEXT (0–4 s):** `Register mein double booking? 😬`
**TEXT (5–8 s):** `Hamara system same slot dobara book hone hi nahi deta ✅`

*Follow-up clip (optional, chain it in Scenebuilder):*
```
Vertical 9:16. SETTING. The RECEPTIONIST calmly types on the laptop, glances at the screen, nods confidently and turns
to a patient with a smile. Clean, bright, steady camera, slow push-in.
She says: "Saade dus wala slot full hai, aapko gyarah baje de deti hoon."
```

## Tips if a clip comes out wrong

- **Wrong language or accent:** add "speaks in Hindi-English (Hinglish) with a natural Indian accent" to the prompt.
- **Faces change between clips:** use *Ingredients to Video* with the same character image, or *Frames to Video* starting from a still of the last clip.
- **Gibberish text on phone screens:** add "the phone screen is not readable, shown from the side" and put the message text in your editor instead.
- **Too cinematic or "fake":** add "smartphone footage, natural lighting, realistic, documentary style".
- **Speech gets cut off:** shorten the line; 8 seconds fits roughly one sentence.
